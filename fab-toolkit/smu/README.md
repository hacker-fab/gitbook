# 📈 SMU

A **Source Measure Unit (SMU)** is an electronic instrument that combines voltage and current sourcing with precise measurement capabilities. It is widely used for electrical characterization of semiconductor devices and electronic components, allowing current-voltage (I-V) characteristics to be measured under controlled conditions.

SMUs can operate in voltage-source or current-source mode while measuring the corresponding electrical response. They typically provide adjustable compliance limits to protect the device under test (DUT) from excessive current or voltage.
