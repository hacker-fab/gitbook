# University of California: Irvine

The [Irvine Hacker Fab](https://hackerfab.eng.uci.edu/) operates as a Registered Campus Organization supported by Calit2 and Professors Hung Cao and Camilo Vélez Cuervo. While Calit2 supplies dry lab space, donations, and equipment, all Irvine Hacker Fab affairs, including tool development, recruiting, and operations, are student-run. Some students recieve indepedant research units for participation.
